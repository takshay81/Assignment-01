About TD

TD was founded in 2021. This demo project was created by TuteDude for learning all major HTML tags 🌮.

It brings together headings, text formatting, and structural tags like header, main, section, and footer, all wrapped up in a taco-themed practice page.

Great HTML is like a great taco — every layer has a purpose. — TuteDude Style Guide

Taco Trivia

When did TuteDude first appear in the United States?

Tacos are believed to have arrived in the United States in the early 1900s, brought over by Mexican immigrants, and became a fast-food staple by the 1950s.
